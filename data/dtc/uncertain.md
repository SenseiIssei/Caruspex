# Generic fault codes left out (uncertain)

Generic SAE J2012 codes that `tools/dtc/extended.csv` does not list yet, and why. They still show
the group meaning of their range in the app (system, severity and, where the range has one, the
plain-language help), so nothing is shown wrong; they just lack the exact text.

A code goes into the table when two independent sources agree on its meaning: the obdex data set
(CC0, https://github.com/foerbsnavi/OBDex, data of 2026-11-07) and a manufacturer document: the GM
OBD descriptions (https://gsi.ext.gm.com/gmspo/mode6/pdf/, 117 documents) or a bulletin or
investigation document in the public NHTSA archive (https://static.nhtsa.gov/odi/, 2171 documents).
The check was done by script on 2026-11-20: the code must appear in the document, and the words of
the part (and every side or position word such as left, rear, intake, upstream) must appear next to it.
Ford's OBD summaries (fordservicecontent.com) refused the download (403) and were not used.

To fill a code: find a second source that names it with the same meaning, add a row to
`tools/dtc/extended.csv` (new phrases go into `tools/dtc/phrases.py` and through
`py -I tools/i18n_rust.py --write --area dtc38`), run `py -I tools/gen_dtc.py` and remove it here.

## Existing entries with disagreeing sources

- **C0040, C0045**: removed. Caruspex listed them as wheel speed sensors right front and left rear (GM's
  use). obdex and NHTSA bulletins from 2018 and 2023 give brake pedal switch A and brake pressure sensor B.
- **C0035**: kept for now, owner to decide. Caruspex and GM: left front wheel speed sensor; obdex: right
  front wheel speed sensor supply. The demo car and several tests use C0035 as a wheel speed fault.

## Left out for wording

- **P22F9, P22FA** (NOx sensor slow response low to high / high to low, bank 1 sensor 2 style): the
  meaning is confirmed, but the composed text would split the direction; needs a phrase of its own.
- **P258A** (vacuum pump control circuit/open): the obdex title is garbled ("Circuit A/Open"); needs a
  phrase of its own.

Some groups in obdex look like guesses rather than SAE codes (for example B0400 to B09FF and
C0100 to C04FF, C0800 to C09FF with titles such as "Audio System Internal Failure"). Only the few

something else on other makes. Be careful with the rest.
