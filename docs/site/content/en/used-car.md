# Used car check

For the viewing before you buy. Take a laptop and an adapter, plug in, and the **Used car** page checks what a seller cannot easily hide.

![Used car check](screenshot-used-car.png)

## What it checks

- **VIN**: does the VIN in the control units match the one in the papers and the catalog?
- **Mileage**: do the odometer readings of the different control units agree?
- **Cleared recently?** Distance and time since the fault memory was last cleared. Cleared shortly before the viewing is a warning sign.
- **Readiness monitors**: are they complete, or were they reset?
- **Stored faults** and the **health check**.
- On electric cars the **battery state** (state of health, cells), where the car reports it.

## The result

A traffic light, the questions to ask the seller and a PDF for the purchase.

## Good to know

- Switch the ignition on, the engine may run. With a cold engine some tests stay open.
- A clean result is no guarantee: OBD sees electronics, not rust, accident damage or worn mechanics. Have the car looked at by a workshop as well.
- Ask the seller before you plug anything in.
