# First steps

## 1. Try the demo car

You do not need a car to look around. On the **Connect** page pick **Demo car**: it drives, warms up, has a few faults stored and answers like a real ELM327. Every page works with it.

## 2. Choose your language and mode

Caruspex follows the language of your system. In the **Settings** you can pick one of 18 languages and the light or dark look; both change at the next start.

The **beginner mode** shows the everyday pages. The **pro mode** adds the workshop tools: the KKL pages, adaptation, raw adapter commands and control unit addresses.

## 3. Connect your car

1. Find the diagnostic socket: the [connect assistant](connect.md) shows where it sits in your car.
2. Plug in the adapter and switch the ignition on. The engine may run, but it does not have to.
3. On the **Connect** page choose the kind of connection, or let Caruspex search all ports by itself.

On the first start the connect assistant opens by itself. Every step can be skipped, and the assistant comes back from the settings and the Connect page.

![The Connect page](screenshot-connect.png)

## 4. A first look at the car

After connecting, Caruspex reads the VIN and tells you which car it is. Good places to start:

- **Health check**: one button, half a minute, a score from 0 to 100 with every lost point explained.
- **What should I do now?**: everything that was found, sorted into three steps.
- The [fault memory](faults.md) and the [live values](live.md).

![Health check](screenshot-health.png)

## 5. Garage and reports

Every car is recognised by its VIN and gets its own entry in the garage, with the history of its checks and odometer readings. The inspection report can be saved as a PDF for the workshop.
