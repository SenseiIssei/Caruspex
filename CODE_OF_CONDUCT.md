# Code of conduct

Caruspex follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
It applies in the issues, pull requests, discussions and every other place of this project, and to everyone taking
part.

In short: be kind and patient, also with people who are new to cars, to programming or to English. Criticise
work, not people. No harassment, insults, sexualised language or personal attacks, and no publishing of other
people's private data, which here includes VINs and number plates.

## Reporting

If someone breaks these rules, report the comment, issue or pull request with GitHub's **Report content** menu
(the three dots next to it). I look at every report that reaches me, keep it confidential and act
on it as the Contributor Covenant describes, from a warning up to a ban from the project.
